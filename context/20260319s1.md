## Key Decisions

1. **Backtest-first approach** – Validate before RT deployment
2. **Uniform conviction scaling** – All regimes scale by conviction (not just contraction)
3. **Conservative contraction** – Even LOW conviction = 40% equity (not 60%)
4. **Monthly retrain** – Walk-forward with monthly model updates

## Risks

- **Over-trading:** Lower conviction → More frequent rebalancing
- **Transaction costs:** Not yet modeled in backtest (assume 5bps)
- **Regime whipsaw:** Low conviction periods may cause churn
