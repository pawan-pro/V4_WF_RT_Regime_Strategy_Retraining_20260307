# V4 Macro Regime Strategy

**An implemented, AI-driven macroeconomic regime classification and asset allocation framework.**

The strategy uses a teacher–student learning framework, with an XGBoost-based student model predicting three market regimes and dynamically allocating capital across QQQ, SPY, and Cash.

The repository contains the strategy's research, development, walk-forward retraining, real-time inference, execution, and monitoring components.

**Current status:** Implemented and live-deployed, with documented historical walk-forward performance of 20.8% CAGR and a 1.0 Sharpe ratio. These historical results are not guarantees of future performance.

**Future research direction:** Explore improvements through knowledge distillation, point-in-time methodology, stronger bias controls, econometric validation, and portfolio optimization, with the objective of achieving more robust risk-adjusted returns.

This work may provide a foundation for future doctoral research in empirical finance and machine learning.
